name: Syllabus Drift Radar scope
description: The product's intended comparison and explicit non-goal.
---

The product compares a college syllabus against job descriptions for a selected role to identify curriculum skill gaps. It is not a tool for comparing two syllabus versions.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the product direction and made version-to-version syllabus comparison a non-goal.

**How to apply:** Keep the workflow focused on role-specific industry skill evidence versus syllabus coverage. Do not reintroduce older/newer syllabus uploads or version-diff features.

Built-in syllabus examples are illustrative demo content and must not be presented as official or verified college curricula.

**Why:** The user explicitly prohibited fabricating institutional curriculum data.

**How to apply:** Keep the illustrative/not-verified label visible anywhere built-in syllabi or their results are shown; only call a syllabus official when a real source has been verified.
